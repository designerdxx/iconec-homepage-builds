Wearing jewellery on rotation is new, so we get a lot of questions.

Here are the common ones. If yours isn’t here, chat with us and someone from the team will answer.

What is Iconec?

A jewellery membership in the UAE. You pay monthly, hold up to three pieces of real diamond jewellery at a time, and swap them whenever you want something new.

Is the jewellery real?

Yes. Every piece is 18k gold set with IGI-certified diamonds.

How many pieces can I hold?

One, two or three at a time, depending on your plan: AED 299, 450 or 599 a month.

When can I swap a piece?

From day 2. Choose the next piece in the app, book a slot, and we collect one and deliver the other.

What happens on day 30?

Each piece is due back by day 30. In its last week you can extend it another 30 days.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

Yes. Choose a value and we send a code they can use when they join.
