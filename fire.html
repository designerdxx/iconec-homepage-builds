Where the idea comes from

A diamond’s fire is white light, split into colour and sent on. Iconec works the same way: real pieces that move from one member to the next, worn, cared for, and worn again.

01.What you wear

Hold one, two or three pieces at a time. Every one is 18k gold set with IGI-certified diamonds.

02.How it moves

Swap any piece from day 2. A courier collects one and hands over the next, at a slot you choose.
