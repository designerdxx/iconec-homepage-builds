How it works

In the app

Choose one, two or three pieces.

Browse what is on rotation and pick how many you want to hold at a time. Every piece is 18k gold set with IGI-certified diamonds.

Day 1

We bring them to you.

Pick a delivery slot that suits you and a courier hands your pieces over in person, anywhere in [delivery area].

Day 2 on

Swap any piece when you want something new.

Choose the next piece in the app and book a slot. We collect one and deliver the other.

Day 30

Send it back, or keep it 30 more days.

Each piece is due by day 30. In its last week, one tap extends it by another 30 days.
