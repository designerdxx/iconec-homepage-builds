How it works

Every piece runs on its own thirty days.

Each piece you hold keeps its own clock, so three pieces can be on three different days.

Day 1Delivered by courier, in person, at the slot you picked.
Day 2 to 22Swap it for another piece whenever you like.
Day 23 to 29Extend it another 30 days, or swap it.
Day 30Due back, unless you extended it.
